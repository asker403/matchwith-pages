This is a small invitation-only trial, with no staffed, around-the-clock moderation team and no guaranteed response time.

Join the friends trial

  1. Install the build using your personal TestFlight invitation when the trial is available.
  2. Sign in with Apple and confirm that you are 18 or older.
  3. Set a name or nickname and age, optionally add a bio and photo, and complete the 24 questions.
  4. Wait for the organizer to approve your invited account.
  5. Answer today’s question before the scheduled matching session.

The matching engine runs during planned sessions. At least two eligible participants are needed; an odd participant may wait. Blocking, prior introductions and daily limits can reduce the available pool.

Save or remove your photo

Open Profile → Edit profile and photo. Choose a photo and check its preview, then tap Save profile changes or Save photo. Simply selecting a photo does not upload it.

Use Remove saved photo to delete your current photo. A photo is optional and is not used in matching. Choose an image you have permission to share.

Conversations and connection problems

Both people accept independently before the 24-hour conversation begins. Reopening the app does not restart the countdown. After the window ends, both people must choose to continue.

Messages refresh while the app is active. Push notifications and background delivery are not part of this trial. If sending is not confirmed, reconnect and use Retry on the same message instead of composing a duplicate.

Block or report

Use the safety menu on an introduction or conversation to report or block. Blocking ends contact; unblocking does not reopen an ended connection.

Profile reports do not attach messages. When reporting a conversation, only messages you explicitly check are included as evidence. You may also email the organizer with a brief description. Avoid sending private chat contents or sensitive screenshots unless you choose to share them for a specific support request.

Report a technical issue

Include your app build number, iOS version, the screen you were using and what happened. Redact private details from screenshots.

Never send passwords, Apple verification codes, account/session tokens or private connection files. We do not need your Apple password to provide support.

Leave the trial

Sign out to remove the device session. Use Delete account in the app to remove your server account, then confirm recent authentication with the same Apple account.

Account deletion is irreversible. Retention and the limits of removing backups or other people’s copies are explained in the privacy notice.
