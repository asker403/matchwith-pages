Join the service

  1. Install Matchwith using the distribution link provided to you.
  2. Sign in with Apple and confirm that you are 18 or older.
  3. Create your profile, choose five interests and your connection preferences, and answer ten questions. Twenty extra questions and a photo are optional.
  4. Wait for account and profile-text approval. Submitted photos are reviewed separately before other people can see them.
  5. Answer today’s question to become eligible for an introduction.

Matching runs automatically every three minutes. At least two eligible participants are needed; an odd participant may wait. Blocking, prior introductions and daily limits can reduce the available pool.

Save or remove your photo

Open Profile → Edit profile. Choose a photo and check its preview, then tap Save profile changes or Save photo. Selecting a photo creates a preview; saving or continuing profile setup with it selected uploads it for review.

Use Remove saved photo to delete your current photo. A photo is optional and is not used in matching. Choose an image you have permission to share.

Conversations and connection problems

Both people accept independently before the 24-hour conversation begins. Reopening the app does not restart the countdown. After the window ends, both people must choose to continue.

Messages refresh while the app is active. You can enable notifications for new messages and introductions. Apple and device settings affect delivery. If sending is not confirmed, reconnect and use Retry on the same message instead of composing a duplicate.

Block or report

Use the safety menu on an introduction or conversation to report or block. Blocking ends contact; unblocking does not reopen an ended connection.

Profile reports do not attach messages. When reporting a conversation, only messages you explicitly check are included as evidence. You may also email support with a brief description. Avoid sending private chat contents or sensitive screenshots unless you choose to share them for a specific support request.

Report a technical issue

Include your app build number, iOS version, the screen you were using and what happened. Redact private details from screenshots.

Never send passwords, Apple verification codes, account/session tokens or private connection files. We do not need your Apple password to provide support.

Leave Matchwith

Sign out to remove the device session. Use Delete account in the app to remove your server account, then confirm recent authentication with the same Apple account.

Account deletion is irreversible. Retention and the limits of removing backups or other people’s copies are explained in the privacy notice.
