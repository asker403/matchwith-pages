Who operates the trial

Matchwith Friends Trial is operated by UMIT BAG, Kağıthane, İstanbul, Türkiye. The app appears on your device as Discovery. It is an invitation-only trial for 2–10 adults aged 18 or older.

For privacy questions or requests, contact umit206403@gmail.com.

Information we use

  • Sign-in information. Apple provides an account identifier and the email you choose to share, which may be an Apple relay address. Supabase handles authentication and session records. We do not receive your Apple password. The app stores session credentials in your device’s Keychain.
  • Your profile. Your name or nickname, self-declared age, optional bio and optional profile photo. A legal name and photo are not required.
  • Your answers and connections. Your 24 discovery answers, daily answers, introduction history and acceptance or continuation decisions.
  • Messages and safety actions. Conversation messages and their timestamps, blocks, reports, messages you explicitly select as report evidence, and limited operational audit records.
  • Service records. Apple, TestFlight and Supabase may process connection, authentication, diagnostic and service records under their applicable terms and privacy notices.

Why we use this information

We use this information to sign you in, save your profile and preferences, choose introductions, operate conversations, respect blocks and participation limits, handle reports, respond to support or privacy requests, and maintain the service.

Matching uses experimental weighted shared-answer rules. Photos and message contents are not used by the matching engine. It is not a validated personality assessment or a prediction of compatibility. The trial has no advertising or analytics SDK added by this app.

Your optional profile photo

The app accesses only the image you select through Apple’s photo picker. Selection creates a preview. Upload occurs only when you choose Save photo, Save profile changes or Save profile and continue with a selected image.

The app makes a small square JPEG, up to 256 KiB, without copying source GPS/EXIF metadata. It does not perform face recognition, biometric matching or liveness checks.

Your photo is stored privately. Only you and eligible introduced participants with an open connection can request it. There is no public photo gallery or permanent public image URL. You can replace or remove it. Blocking, ending contact or account restrictions prevent subsequent authorized reads.

Someone who has already seen your photo may have captured or saved it. We cannot recall their screenshots or other copies. Displayed photo data is kept in memory and cleared when the view becomes inactive, disappears or loses access.

Who can see your information

An introduced participant can see your name or nickname, age, bio, available photo and a shared-answer explanation. They do not receive your full private answer list or your private continuation choice. Conversation participants can read messages while the service’s access rules permit.

A profile report attaches no conversation messages. A conversation report can include up to 20 messages you explicitly select. Unselected messages are not automatically attached. The report-review tools expose selected evidence rather than a general private-chat browser.

The matching worker cannot read photos, chats or report evidence. Infrastructure administrators retain technical database access. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. The small trial does not have a staffed, around-the-clock moderation team.

Services and international hosting

The app uses Apple sign-in and Supabase. The configured Supabase project is hosted in West EU (Ireland). For participants in Türkiye and the United States, app information is therefore processed outside their country.

Participation is voluntary. Avoid sensitive profile details and private contact information. Contact the operator before joining if you need information about the trial’s processing or transfer arrangements.

Retention and account deletion

  • Your profile, answers and current photo remain while your account exists unless you remove an item or delete your account. Removing a photo deletes its active record.
  • Messages in ended connections, or expired connections that were not mutually continued, become eligible for cleanup after seven days. Mutually continued conversations remain until ended or the account is deleted.
  • Reports and their copied evidence become eligible for cleanup after 30 days; operational audit events after 90 days. Audit events do not contain message bodies or answer lists.
  • An hourly cleanup job removes eligible records. These periods are not exact-to-the-second deletion promises. An account-deletion audit event can remain within the audit period.
  • Provider backups, transaction logs and copies already received by other participants have separate lifetimes. Removing an active record does not guarantee immediate removal from every backup or copy.

Use Delete account in the app to remove your server account. It requires recent authentication with the same Apple account. Signing out removes the device session but does not delete the account. The fictional offline development demo is separate from your hosted trial account.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can use a nickname, leave the photo and bio empty, edit your profile, decline an introduction, end contact, block, report, sign out or delete your account.

Contact umit206403@gmail.com to ask about access, correction, deletion or other privacy rights that apply to you. Do not send passwords, Apple verification codes, identity tokens or unnecessary sensitive information. We may need to confirm that a request concerns your own account.

Age is self-declared. Apple sign-in and choosing a photo do not verify a person’s age or identity. The trial is intended only for invited adults aged 18 or older.
